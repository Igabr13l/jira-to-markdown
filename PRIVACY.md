# Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

Jira Ticket to Markdown processes the contents and URL of the Jira Cloud tab you explicitly activate it on. Processing occurs locally in your browser to create an editable Markdown preview.

The extension does not transmit, sell, share, retain, or use Jira ticket content, URLs, selected text, or personal information outside your browser. It has no analytics, advertising, accounts, or external servers. Your character-limit preference is stored locally through Chrome extension storage.

The extension only receives temporary access to the active tab when you invoke it. It does not read other tabs or other Jira tickets. Copying Markdown places the reviewed text on your system clipboard at your request.

You are responsible for reviewing confidential information before pasting it into another service. Changes to this policy will be published with the extension listing and in this repository.

Questions can be submitted through the public [support tracker](https://github.com/Igabr13l/jira-to-markdown/issues).
